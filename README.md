## Income

A friendly calculator that tells you how much of your investment returns you can spend each month without shrinking your wealth. Enter your invested amount, monthly return and income tax rate, and Income sets aside enough to keep up with inflation using live IPCA data from IBGE, so you know exactly what is safe to live on.
